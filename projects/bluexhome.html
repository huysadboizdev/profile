01 / OVERVIEW

The product and its purpose.

BlueXHome is a personal finance web platform that brings income, expenses, accounts, budgets and cash flow into one place. The product helps people organize transactions and understand their financial picture.

I worked on the platform at NETVIET Technology and Media JSC as a full-time Full-stack Developer, contributing across frontend and backend in a team of three.

01

Money in, money out

Transaction tracking, account management and categorization of income and expenses.

02

Planning ahead

Budgeting, recurring payments and bill reminders to support day-to-day financial organization.

03

A wider financial picture

Financial reports, cash-flow views and financial calculation tools.

04

Working with your data

Multi-currency support and data import/export for flexible financial record management.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Working across the stack.

My role covered both frontend and backend development. I worked on feature integration and connected application views to backend APIs as part of the team's shared delivery.

  • Contributed to core financial management features, including transaction tracking, accounts, budgeting and reports.
  • Worked with React Query and Zustand on frontend state management and API integration.
  • Used Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui for the interface, with attention to responsiveness and maintainability.
  • Contributed to multi-currency, data import/export and financial calculation functionality.
Collaborative delivery. This work was delivered by a three-person team. My role included both frontend and backend contributions.
