01 / OVERVIEW

The product and its purpose.

Love PDF is a full-stack document platform offering more than 20 PDF tools. Its scope includes merging, splitting, compression, conversion, editing, OCR and document organization.

I contributed to the platform at NETVIET Technology and Media JSC as a full-time Full-stack Developer in a team of three, working on both frontend and backend.

01

Organize documents

Merge, split, crop, rotate and manage pages within PDF files.

02

Convert formats

Conversion workflows between PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, WebP and HTML.

03

Edit & annotate

Editing, e-signatures and watermarking as part of the document toolset.

04

Process & protect

Compression, OCR, password protection and unlocking workflows.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Working across the stack.

My work spanned the frontend experience and backend functionality of the document processing platform. The product was developed collaboratively, with a shared focus on the complete file-handling workflow.

  • Contributed to the platform's PDF toolset and multi-format document conversion features.
  • Worked on file upload, processing and download workflows.
  • Implemented validation and error handling within file-processing flows.
  • Contributed to editing, document organization and protection features as part of the team.
Collaborative delivery. This work was delivered by a three-person team. My role included both frontend and backend contributions.
